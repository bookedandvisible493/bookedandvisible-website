Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026 · Booked & Visible is operated from British Columbia, Canada. Contact: hello@bookedandvisible.ca

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information Booked & Visible collects when you visit our website or purchase our products, how we use it, and the choices available to you. It applies alongside our Terms of Service.

2. Information we collect

When you purchase or contact us, we collect information such as your name, email address, and business details (business name, service area, phone number) where relevant to the product purchased. If you purchase the Landing Page Add-on, we also collect any photos, testimonials, or additional business information you choose to submit for that page. Payment information (such as your card details) is collected and processed directly by our payment platform and its payment processors — we do not receive or store your full card number.

When you place an order, we also collect the business information you submit in our order form (for example your website, Google Business Profile link, services, and the searches your customers use). Your reports and other deliverables are sent to you by email, and we keep a record of when each one was sent, together with your payment receipt and order record, so we can confirm delivery and answer questions about your order.

If you use the contact form on our website to ask a question before purchasing, we collect the email address and message you submit, and use a third-party form-processing service to deliver it to us.

We maintain an internal customer and order record — your contact and business details, which product and plan you're on, and a record of your orders and any support requests — so we can answer questions about your account quickly and keep your service consistent. This record draws on the same information described above; it does not involve collecting anything new from you.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to deliver the products you purchase, prepare your personalized onboarding report using publicly available information about your business (such as your Google Business Profile, website, and reviews), send you transactional emails about your order, and respond to your support requests. We use our order and delivery records described in Section 2 to confirm that your order was delivered and, where necessary, to resolve payment disputes. We use AI tools (such as Anthropic's Claude), operated by us, to help research and draft your reports from the information you submit and publicly available information; a person reviews every report before it is sent to you. We only send marketing emails with your consent (including implied consent as an existing customer), consistent with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), and every marketing email includes a way to unsubscribe. Emails about your order are not marketing.

4. Who we share information with

We use a small number of third-party service providers to run our business: Stripe (checkout and payment processing), Payhip (checkout and file delivery for the Starter Kit), MailerLite (email communications), Cloudflare (website hosting), Google Analytics (website usage analytics — see Section 7), Meta (Meta Pixel, website and advertising analytics — see Section 7), Airtable (our internal customer and order record described in Section 2), a form-processing service (FormSubmit, based in the United States) for our website's contact and order forms, our email provider, and AI service providers (such as Anthropic) that process the information needed to prepare your reports. To test how AI assistants describe your business, we enter your business name and your chosen search phrases into ChatGPT (OpenAI), Perplexity, Gemini and Google Search. If you add us as a Manager on your Google Business Profile, we can see your profile's reviews and messages; we use that access only to do the work you ordered, and you can remove it at any time. These providers process information on our behalf to deliver our services and have their own privacy practices governing that processing. Access to our internal customer record is restricted to the systems and individuals who need it to run the business — it is not set up for public or shared access. We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our products, respond to support requests, and meet our own legal, tax, and accounting record-keeping obligations. As a general rule, if you have had no order or contact with us for 3 years, we archive or delete your record from our internal customer record, whichever is appropriate to our legal and accounting obligations at that time.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing hello@bookedandvisible.ca. As a British Columbia business, we're primarily governed by BC's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA); the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) also applies to the extent your information is transferred to service providers based outside Canada (see Section 9). We extend the same access, correction, and deletion rights to all customers regardless of location.

7. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website — pages viewed, general location (city/region level), device type, and how you arrived at our site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this, and information collected is processed by Google in accordance with their own privacy policy. We do not use this data to individually identify you.

We also use the Meta Pixel (Meta/Facebook) on our website. It works similarly to Google Analytics — recording website activity such as pages viewed — and helps us understand the effectiveness of, and build audiences for, any advertising we run on Meta's platforms (Facebook and Instagram). Information collected through the Meta Pixel is processed by Meta in accordance with their own privacy policy, and Meta's own use of that information is governed by Meta's terms and policies rather than ours. If we begin running paid advertising (e.g. Google Ads or Meta ads) in the future, we will update this section to describe any additional cookies or tracking used for that purpose before it goes live.

Analytics cookies — including both Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel — are off by default. On your first visit to a page that uses them, a banner asks whether to turn them on; declining keeps them off, and the site works the same either way. When analytics are declined, Google may still receive cookieless, aggregated signals (Google Consent Mode) that can't identify you, and the Meta Pixel script loads but sends no events. You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer.

8. Children's privacy

Our products are intended for business owners and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

9. International users

Booked & Visible operates from Canada. Because some of our service providers are based in the United States, your information may be processed there as well as in Canada.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted with an updated "last updated" date above.

11. Contact

Booked & Visible is a British Columbia sole proprietorship (Registration FM1114995). Our designated Privacy Officer under BC's Personal Information Protection Act can be reached at hello@bookedandvisible.ca. Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your personal information, can be sent to the same address.
