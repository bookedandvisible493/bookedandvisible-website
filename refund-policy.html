Refund Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

The simple rule for every product

You can cancel for a full refund any time before we send you your first deliverable (your report, your fixes, or your website preview). After that, the purchase is final — except in the cases below, which apply to every product.

If we're late or can't deliver what's described: if we miss the delivery time stated for your product, or can't deliver something the product page describes, you choose — a full refund, or we fix it at no charge.

Starter Kit

The six guides and templates are delivered by email within minutes of purchase. Your personalized onboarding report follows within 1 business day of receiving your intake form (the link is on the Starter Kit page and in your receipt). You can cancel for a full refund until your personalized report is sent. If any file is missing or unreadable, email hello@bookedandvisible.ca and we'll fix it right away.

Visibility Report

Refundable until your Report is sent (usually within 2 business days of your intake form). Your Report includes real answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI results. If we ever have to send it before those answers are captured, the Report will say so, your score is marked provisional, and your refund window stays open until we send the live answers.

AI Visibility Boost and SEO Improvement Package

Refundable until we send your first deliverable (your Report and fixes). After delivery we re-check your 0–100 score at 30 days. If it hasn't risen by at least 5 points — measured with the same searches and AI assistants as your first report — we find out why, redo any fix that didn't take, and re-check once more at 60 days, free. We can't guarantee any specific ranking, calls or revenue (see our Terms).

Report credit

If you buy a Visibility Report and upgrade to the AI Visibility Boost or the SEO Improvement Package within 30 days of receiving your Report, the full $79 CAD is credited toward the upgrade. Email hello@bookedandvisible.ca before you upgrade and we'll send you a credit code to use at checkout.

Landing Pages

Refundable until we send you your website preview. After that, one round of revisions is included within 7 days. If you bought the domain-included option and we've already registered your domain, the domain is yours to keep and the $60 domain portion of the price is not refundable.

If you never send your intake form

We'll remind you twice. If we still haven't heard from you 30 days after purchase, we'll refund you in full automatically.

Before you request a chargeback

If something isn't right with your order, please contact us directly at hello@bookedandvisible.ca first — most issues can be resolved quickly by email. By purchasing from Booked & Visible, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Refund Policy.
