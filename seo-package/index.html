Do I need to give you access to my website?

No. Website changes — page titles, descriptions, headings, schema, and FAQ content — are delivered ready to paste in, page by page, so you or whoever manages your site can add them. How fast will I see results?

Everything is delivered within 7 business days of your intake form. Google, directories, and AI engines re-crawl on their own schedule, usually 1–6 weeks, so we send you a before/after report at 30 days rather than promising an overnight change.

If your 30-day report shows no measurable movement, we re-run the fixes at no charge.
