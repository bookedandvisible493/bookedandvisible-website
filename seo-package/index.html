Common questions

Who is this for?

Independent local businesses that already have a website but don't show up when customers search Google, Google Maps, or ask an AI assistant. No website yet? Pair a Landing Page with this package.

How is this different from the AI Visibility Boost?

The Boost fixes how AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity understand your business. The SEO Improvement Package includes everything in the Boost, plus your Google Business Profile, the pages of your website, help with key Canadian directories (including Bing, Apple Maps, Yelp and YellowPages), and a review system — the full set of signals Google and AI search both use.

Do I need to give you access to my website?

No. Website changes — page titles, descriptions, headings, schema, and FAQ content — are delivered ready to paste in, page by page, so you or whoever manages your site can add them. If we built your site, we make the changes directly. For your Google Business Profile, you add us as a manager from your own account; you never share a password.

How fast will I see results?

Everything is delivered within 7 business days of your intake form and Google Business Profile access. Directories that need you to confirm a code can take a little longer — we keep you posted. Google, directories, and AI engines re-crawl on their own schedule, usually 1–6 weeks, and visible movement in local results can take a few months, so we send you a before/after report at 30 days rather than promising an overnight change.

Will I need to do anything for the directory listings?

Sometimes. A few directories, like Apple Maps, Bing, and Yelp, only let the business owner register or verify, often with a code sent to your business phone or email. We handle what we can, then give you step-by-step directions for the rest and tell you when a code is coming so you can forward it to us. Which directories apply depends on your industry and location.

What if my score doesn't improve?

At 30 days we re-check using the same searches and AI assistants as your first report and email you a before/after report that explains what moved, what didn't, and why. Some things depend on you, like a directory code you haven't forwarded yet or website changes you haven't pasted in, and the report flags those so you know what is still open. We can't guarantee a specific score increase, rankings, calls or revenue.

Who is this not for?

No website yet? Start with a landing page. Looking for ads, social media management or monthly content? That isn't this package. And if you want a guaranteed ranking, we can't promise one, and neither can anyone honest.

Can you use my results as an example?

Only if you want to. We'd love to share your before/after score as an example (with or without your business name). It's optional and doesn't change your price.
