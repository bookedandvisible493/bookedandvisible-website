How Booked & Visible started

Booked & Visible began in the summer of 2026. Victor was helping with the website for a family business, a hair studio, that wasn't getting traffic. To find out why, he spent time learning how Google search works, what helps AI assistants find a website, how to make pages easier to read, and how to make a site simple to use. Fixing the gaps he found brought good results.

He wanted to help other small local businesses in the same position: people who are very good at what they do, but don't have the time or the technical know-how to set up a website that gets them found and booked. That's where the name and the service come from. Booked & Visible is for local trades, medical and dental clinics and other businesses that rely on their website to stay visible to customers nearby.

That first project shaped how we work: instead of generic "local services" advice, we build around the daily reality of one specific business. That approach has since opened up to other independent, local businesses. Booked & Visible currently also works with a denture and implant clinic and an equestrian boarding facility, and every job is built around the specific business, not a template with the name swapped in.
