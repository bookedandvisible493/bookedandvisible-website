Common questions

Who is this for?

Independent local businesses that already have a Google Business Profile and want it kept fresh, answered and accurate every month without doing it themselves. It works best after an AI Visibility Boost or SEO Improvement Package, but you don't need one first.

Is there a contract?

No. Stay Visible is month to month. Cancel any time by replying to any of our emails or writing to hello@bookedandvisible.ca, and your plan ends at the end of the month you've paid for. Nothing is held back: every post, reply and listing stays yours.

Do you need my Google password?

No. You add us as a Manager on your Google Business Profile from your own account, and you can remove us any time. We never ask for passwords.

Who writes the posts and review replies?

We do, using your services, your area and photos you send us. Replies to reviews of 3 stars or less are sent to you for a yes before we post them.

How is the score checked every month?

Each month we re-check the parts of your 0–100 score we can verify from public sources: your Google profile, business details, directories and reviews. Every 3 months we also re-ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI the same searches as your first check, so the numbers stay comparable.

What isn't included?

New directory listings, website changes, new pages and paid ads. If you need one of those, we quote it as a one-time job first. You never get billed for extra work you didn't approve.

What does the founding rate mean?

Our first 20 Stay Visible clients pay $79 CAD a month instead of $99, for as long as they stay subscribed.

Can I get a refund?

Your first month is refundable in full until we send your first deliverable. After that, you can cancel any time and won't be charged again, but a month already started isn't refunded. Full details are in our Refund Policy.
