Setting up your domain

Once your landing page is built, there's one last step to make it live at your own domain. Which steps apply to you depends on which tier you purchased — jump to the right section below.

Bring Your Own Domain

For this tier, you already have a domain (or you've bought one on your own from a registrar like Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Google Domains). Once your page is built, we email you the exact DNS records to add — here's what that process looks like:

  1. We send you your DNS records. Once your page is ready, you'll get an email with the specific record type (usually a CNAME, sometimes an A record depending on your setup), the host/name value, and the target value to enter.
  2. Log in to your domain registrar — the company you bought or renewed your domain through — and find its DNS management page. This is usually called "DNS Settings," "Manage DNS," or "Advanced DNS," depending on your registrar.
  3. Add the record we sent you exactly as written. Most registrars have an "Add Record" button; paste the values into the matching fields and save.
  4. Wait for it to propagate. DNS changes typically take effect within a few hours, though it can occasionally take up to 24–48 hours depending on your registrar.
  5. We confirm it's live and let you know once your page is reachable at your domain.

Not sure where your DNS settings are, or what a field means? Reply to our setup email or write to hello@bookedandvisible.ca and we'll walk you through your specific registrar's screen.

All-In, Domain Included

For this tier, we register your domain and connect it to your page ourselves — you don't need to touch any DNS settings. Once it's confirmed live, ownership transfers to you:

  1. We choose and register your domain based on your business name and service area, and connect it to your new page — no action needed from you.
  2. We confirm it's live and email you once your page is reachable at your new domain.
  3. We send you a free-account invite (through our domain registrar) to accept ownership of the domain — this is the "one confirmation click" mentioned in your product description.
  4. Click to accept and the domain is fully and permanently yours from that point on, including responsibility for renewal from year two onward (about $12–15/year, billed to you directly by the registrar — not by us).

Didn't get the transfer email, or something looks off? Write to hello@bookedandvisible.ca and we'll sort it out.
