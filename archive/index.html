FAQ

Questions you might have

Not a plumber?

We're plumbing-only for now — the guides, credentials, and content are all built specifically around that trade. If you're in a different trade and think this could work for your business too, tell us — we're tracking interest before deciding what's next.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. Everything is written in plain language with step-by-step instructions. If you can use a smartphone, you can implement this.

How long until I see results?

Most of the Starter Kit's items — like optimizing your Google Business Profile — can be done the same week. Visibility improvements typically build over the following weeks as reviews and photos accumulate.

What if I don't have a website?

That's fine — the Starter Kit doesn't require one. You can add a custom landing page to any purchase: $329 CAD (≈$249 USD) if you already have (or get) your own domain, or $389 CAD (≈$289 USD) if you'd rather we register a domain and hand it fully over to you. Booked members get it at a discounted price ($199 CAD / ≈$150 USD) after two months of membership (right away for annual members). Full details: What's Included.

How is this different from Angi, Thumbtack, or a directory listing?

Nothing is ever held hostage — any page we build for you ends up on a domain you own outright, even on our top tier, and you can leave any time. Compare that to Angi, Thumbtack, and similar directories: they charge per lead, often $20-120 each whether or not the job actually books, and typically lock you into a listing you don't control. Booked & Visible is a flat, predictable price with no per-lead fees.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, any time — no contracts, no cancellation fees.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, any time before your personalized report has been generated — usually within 24 hours of purchase. Once your report is delivered, the purchase is final, since at that point you've received the personalized analysis. If a file is missing or broken, or your report never arrives, contact us and we'll fix it or refund it — no exceptions there. Full details in our Refund Policy.
