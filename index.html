Questions people ask

What is in the $79 AI Visibility Report?

A 0-100 score built from real ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI answers about your business, a prioritized fix list and a 90-day plan, delivered within 2 business days of your intake form.

Should I start with the Report or go straight to Boost?

Boost and the SEO Package both include the Report. If you already know you want the fixes, start with Boost or the SEO Package and you will not pay for a Report separately. If you would rather see your score first, start with the $79 Report.

What if my score does not go up?

If your Boost or SEO 30-day re-check score has not risen by at least 5 points, we redo what did not take and re-check free at 60 days. We do not guarantee rankings, calls or revenue.

Do I need a website for the SEO Package?

Yes, the SEO Package works on an existing website. If you have none, landing pages start at $329 for one page.

How long does each project take?

The Report takes 2 business days from your intake form, Boost 5 business days, the SEO Package 7 business days from intake and profile access. Landing pages go live 3, 5 or 7 business days after we receive your content.

Are there contracts or refunds?

No contracts and no per-lead fees. Every project is refundable before the first deliverable, and if we miss a delivery time you get a refund or a free fix. Stay Visible is month to month.
