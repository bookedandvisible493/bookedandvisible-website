Questions

Business card design FAQ

How much does custom business card design cost?

$99 CAD, one flat price. It includes 5 original front-and-back designs, one round of changes on the design you pick, a scan-tested QR code, and print-ready files. Printing is paid separately to the printer you choose.

How is this different from a Canva or Vistaprint template?

A template is a layout thousands of other businesses can use, and you fill it in yourself. We design 5 options around your own logo, colours and services, check your details against your Google Business Profile and website, and set up the files for your printer so nothing gets cut off.

How long does it take?

You get 5 concepts within 1 business day of sending the intake form. After you pick one and send your changes, the revised card comes back within 1 business day, and the final files are sent the same business day you approve it.

I don't have a logo. Can you still design my card?

Yes. In the intake form we ask what you'd like to do and give you options: a clean text logo set from your business name (included), working from the best image you already have, such as your sign or social media picture, or pausing your order while you get a logo made.

Which printers do the files work with?

Any printer. You tell us where you plan to print (Vistaprint, Staples, a local print shop or another service) and we set up the files for that printer's card size and bleed, with step-by-step upload instructions.

Can the QR code go to my Google reviews instead of my website?

Yes. The QR code can open your website, your booking page or your Google review link. Links to your website or booking page get a tracking tag, so your website statistics can show how many visits came from your cards. Google review links can't carry a tracking tag, so the sign those cards are working is new reviews coming in. We scan-test every QR code before sending the files. Need the review link? See our guide to finding your Google review link and QR code.

How do I see how many people used the QR code on my card?

If the QR code points to your website or booking page, the visits show up in your website statistics. In Google Analytics, open Reports, then Acquisition, then Traffic acquisition, switch the table to Session source / medium, and look for card / print. Wix, Squarespace and Shopify show it in their own traffic reports. It counts website visits rather than scans, so treat it as a minimum. No analytics yet? The tag starts counting as soon as you add analytics, with no reprint needed.

Do you print the cards?

No. You get print-ready files and order the printing yourself, so you choose the paper, quantity and price. Printing 250 standard cards usually costs between about $25 and $80 CAD.

Who owns the design?

You do. You get the final files to reprint wherever and whenever you like, with no extra fees.
