Where to ask for reviews beyond Google

Google is the platform most local searches run through, so it should get the bulk of your review-asking effort. But depending on your business, a second platform can genuinely be worth building too.

Why Google should still come first

Most local search traffic and the map pack itself run through Google reviews specifically, so a request that isn't clearly pointed at Google is often a wasted ask. Get this one working well before splitting attention.

When a second platform is worth it

Facebook can matter for businesses whose customers are already active there and where community trust and shares carry weight. Industry-specific platforms sometimes matter too, depending on your field — the right choice depends on where your specific customers already look, not on collecting reviews everywhere for its own sake.

The mistake of asking for the same review everywhere at once

Sending one customer a request for a Google review, a Facebook review, and a third platform all at once usually gets you none of them — the ask feels like a chore instead of a quick favor, and most people abandon it partway through.

A simple rotation instead

Ask for a Google review by default. If a second platform genuinely matters for your business, alternate who you ask, or wait until Google is in a healthy, steady place before splitting any attention toward a second platform.
