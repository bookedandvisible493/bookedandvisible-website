Google AI Overviews: what they mean for a local business

On many searches, Google now shows an AI-written summary at the top of the page, called an AI Overview, before the usual map and links. There's also AI Mode, a chat-style version of Search. For a local business, that summary can be the first thing a customer reads, so it's worth knowing what it draws on.

What an AI Overview is

An AI Overview is a short answer Google writes for the search, with links to the sources it used. It doesn't appear on every search; Google shows it when it thinks a summary helps. For local questions such as "best time to reseal a driveway in Surrey" or "do I need a permit for a deck in Langley", the Overview often names or links to businesses and pages it considers trustworthy.

What Google's AI draws on for local searches

Google hasn't published exactly what its AI uses for local searches, but it says the normal Search fundamentals apply, with no special optimization or markup required. These are the signals that matter:

  • Your Google Business Profile: category, services, description, hours, reviews and your replies.
  • Your website: clear service pages, your service area in plain words, and answers to real questions.
  • Structured data (schema markup) that labels your business type, services and location. It isn't required for AI features, but it helps Google understand your pages.
  • Consistency and reputation across the web: matching details on directories, and recent reviews.

In other words, the same foundations that help you in the map results (see how Google decides which business shows up first) also feed the AI summary.

Practical steps

  1. Finish every field of your profile using our Google Business Profile checklist, or have us do it with the SEO Improvement Package.
  2. Write a short FAQ on your site that answers the questions customers actually ask you on the phone, in plain language. Pages that answer questions directly are clearer for customers and search engines alike.
  3. Add structured data for your business type and services. Only mark up what's visible on the page.
  4. Keep reviews coming and reply to them. Our review reply templates make that quick.
  5. Check the results for your own key searches every month or two, since Overviews change often.

Can you see AI Overview traffic in Search Console?

Google counts clicks and impressions from AI Overviews and AI Mode inside the normal Performance report in Search Console (under the "Web" search type), rather than in a separate AI report. For a small or new site with little search traffic, there may simply be nothing to show yet. It's also useful to check what the assistants say directly, as described in how to get recommended by AI assistants.

Quick answers

Can I opt in to AI Overviews?

No. Google decides when to show an Overview and which sources to use. You improve your odds by keeping your profile, website and listings complete, accurate and consistent.

Will AI Overviews take away my customers?

They change where people click, but for local services people still need to call or book a real business. Being one of the businesses the summary mentions matters more than ever.

Does blocking AI crawlers in robots.txt stop AI Overviews using my site?

No. AI Overviews are part of Google Search, which uses Googlebot, and blocking Googlebot would remove you from Search entirely. To limit what Google shows from a page, Google points to the nosnippet and max-snippet settings, which also affect normal search snippets.
