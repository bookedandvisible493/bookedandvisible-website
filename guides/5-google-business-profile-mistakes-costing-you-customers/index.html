5 Google Business Profile mistakes that are costing you customers

Most independent local businesses already have a Google Business Profile. Very few have one that's actually working for them. These five mistakes are the most common reasons a profile underperforms — and each one is fixable in an afternoon.

01

An incomplete or wrong primary category

Google leans heavily on your primary category to decide which searches you show up for. A category that's close but not exact — or one left as a generic default — quietly excludes you from the searches that actually convert. Pick the most specific category that matches what you do, and add secondary categories for everything else.

02

Stale or missing photos

Profiles with recent, real photos get more clicks and more direction requests than ones with a single logo image from years ago. Aim for a handful of new photos every month — the work itself, the team, the space — not just a one-time upload.

03

Ignoring reviews, or leaving them unanswered

An unanswered review — good or bad — signals a business that isn't paying attention. A short, genuine reply to every review takes a few minutes and tells both Google and future customers that someone's home.

04

Inconsistent name, address, and phone number

If your business shows up with a slightly different name, an old address, or a different phone number on other directories, it undermines the trust signals Google uses to rank your profile. Keep this information identical everywhere it appears.

05

No regular posts or updates

A profile that never posts looks inactive, even if the business behind it is thriving. Short, simple updates — a finished project, a seasonal note, a promotion — keep the profile active and give searchers a reason to choose you over a competitor.
