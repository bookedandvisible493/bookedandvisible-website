How to get your Google review link and QR code (and where to use them)

Many happy customers never get round to leaving a review, often because finding your business on Google and then the review button takes too many taps. A direct review link takes them straight to the star rating. Google gives you one for free, along with a QR code.

Quick answer

Sign in to your Google Business Profile on a computer, choose Read reviews, then Get more reviews, and copy the short link or download the QR code. Text the link right after each job, add it to your invoice email, and put the QR code on a printed card or at your counter, and ask every customer, not just the happy ones.

Where to find your review link

  1. Sign in to the Google account that manages your Business Profile, ideally on a computer. Google's own help notes that the review QR code is only available in a desktop browser.
  2. Search your business name on Google to open your profile controls, or open Business Profile Manager.
  3. Choose Read reviews, then Get more reviews (some accounts show "Ask for reviews" instead).
  4. Copy the short link. On the same screen you can download the QR code.

Test the link on your phone once before you print anything.

Where to use it

  • By text, right after the job. This usually works best: "Thanks again today. If you have a minute, a quick review really helps: [link]".
  • On your invoice or receipt email, as one short line near the bottom.
  • A printed card with the QR code to leave behind, at your counter, or on your vehicle.
  • In your email signature and on your website's contact page.

For timing and wording that doesn't feel pushy, see how to get more reviews without feeling awkward. The SEO Improvement Package includes a printable QR card and request scripts made for your business.

Stay within the rules

  • Ask every customer, not only the ones you expect to be happy. Filtering who gets asked (sometimes called review gating) is against Google's policies.
  • Don't offer discounts, draws or gifts for reviews.
  • Don't ask staff or family to review you.

In Canada, fake or incentivized reviews can also breach the Competition Act, and texts or emails asking for reviews should identify your business. More detail on Google's rules is in is it legal to ask customers for reviews. And don't forget the other places customers look, covered in where to ask for reviews beyond Google.

Common questions

Is the Google review link free?

Yes. It comes with your Google Business Profile. You don't need a paid tool to get or share it.

Why can't I find the QR code on my phone?

Google's help says the review QR code is only available in a desktop browser. Use a computer, or share the link and make a QR code from it.

How many reviews should I aim for?
