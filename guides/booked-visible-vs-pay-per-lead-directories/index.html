Booked & Visible vs. pay-per-lead directories: what it actually costs

If you run an independent local business, you've probably looked at Angi, Thumbtack, or a similar lead directory. They put you in front of people searching right now — and take a cut of every job, whether it turns into real work or not. Here's what each option actually costs, what you own at the end of it, and where a flat-fee, ownership-based approach fits.

How pay-per-lead directories actually work

Platforms like Angi and Thumbtack sell leads, not jobs. You pay for contact information from someone who filled out a form — not for a booked customer. A few things follow from that:

  • You pay whether or not the lead ever answers the phone.
  • The same lead is often sold to several businesses at once, so you're paying for a chance to quote, not the job itself.
  • Lead prices rise with demand — during storms, holidays, or peak season, exactly when you'd want costs to stay predictable.
  • When you stop paying, your visibility on the platform disappears with it. You don't own anything.

Which setup fits your situation

  • You want fast, one-off leads and don't mind paying per contact: a directory platform can supplement your pipeline, especially early on or in a slow season.
  • You want a Google Business Profile and local search presence that compounds over time, without per-lead fees: the Starter Kit gets your profile and review system in place in one pass.
  • You want ongoing content and seasonal updates without doing it yourself: Visible keeps your profile and social presence current every month.
  • You want your own landing page working alongside your Google presence, or don't have a website yet: Booked adds a page you own outright, refreshed quarterly — see Landing Pages.

Many businesses run a directory platform and an owned presence side by side during the transition, then scale back the per-lead spend once their own profile starts carrying more of the load.

FAQ

Is Booked & Visible only for one type of business?

No. It started with plumbing and now works with any independent, local business that competes on being found first — home service trades, clinics, and recreation or agriculture businesses among them.

Do I need technical skills to set this up?

No. The Starter Kit and memberships are step-by-step guides and templates, not code or design work.

How is this different from a directory listing?

Directory listings like Angi and Thumbtack charge per lead and control the platform you're listed on. Booked & Visible helps you build and own your Google Business Profile and web presence directly, for a flat fee with no per-lead cost.

Can I cancel a membership if it's not working for me?

Yes — both membership tiers can be cancelled any time, with no long-term contract.

How long until I see results switching from a lead platform to an owned profile?

Most initial setup work, like optimizing a Google Business Profile, shows up in local search within the first few weeks. Ranking improvements build over months, similar to any local SEO effort.
