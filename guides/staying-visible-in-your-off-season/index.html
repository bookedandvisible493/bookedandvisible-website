Staying visible in your off-season

Plenty of independent businesses run on a seasonal curve — busy stretches followed by real slow periods. Going quiet during the slow months is the natural instinct, and it's usually the wrong one.

Quick answer

Keep your profile active through the slow months: finish photos and services, ask the season's customers for reviews, and post simple seasonal updates. Businesses that do this tend to start the next busy season ahead of competitors who went quiet.

Why going quiet costs you at the start of next season

A profile that stops posting, stops collecting reviews, and stops getting attention for months looks less active exactly when the next busy season starts and competitors who kept working through the slow period are already ahead on prominence.

What to actually do with the slack time

  • Catch up on profile completeness — photos, service list, description — work that's easy to put off when you're busy.
  • Reach back out to the season's customers for reviews while the work is still fresh in their memory.
  • Plan out a simple content calendar for the months ahead, so posting doesn't rely on remembering to do it in the moment.

Posting through the off-season without sounding out of season

Off-season posts don't need to pretend business is booming — a seasonal reminder, a behind-the-scenes update, or a “booking now for [next season]” post all keep a profile looking active without overstating anything.

Getting ahead before demand returns

Businesses that use the slow months to build reviews and tighten up their profile tend to come into the next busy season already ranking ahead of the competitors who let things go quiet — the gap shows up right when it matters most.
